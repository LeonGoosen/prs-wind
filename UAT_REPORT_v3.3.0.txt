ENGINE v2 ARCHITECTURE:
- Ballistic solver MIN/MAX holds remain the ballistic truth layer.
- Wind evidence is fused probabilistically rather than by a single midpoint heuristic.
- Direction fusion is circular, so 359° and 1° are treated as adjacent.
- Visual 'moving TO' observations convert to WIND FROM before fusion.
- Speed and direction uncertainty are propagated through target DOF.
- 6,000 deterministic Monte Carlo wind states are evaluated per target.
- Required hold distribution is optimized on the shooter's real 0.1-mil hold grid.
- Target horizontal angular width determines modeled wind-hit coverage.
- Robust Hold is the 0.1-mil plateau retaining at least 95% of maximum modeled wind-hit probability.
- Confidence is separate from Wind Hit and scores evidence agreement/quantity, speed uncertainty, terrain and calibration status.

IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC LIMITATION:
- The current trajectory-zone weighting remains an explicit interim approximation because the app does not yet have sufficient rifle/ammo solver perturbation data to derive a true trajectory wind-sensitivity kernel.
- Wind Hit is therefore a modeled wind-window probability, not a validated total hit probability.
